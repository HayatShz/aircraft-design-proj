BEECHCRAFT STARSHIP PRELIMINARY MODEL

Published/proposal dimensions are used for baseline values.
Main wing uses an equivalent trapezoid approximation.
Main-wing taper ratio and sweep are assumed.
Canard span and area are approximate reference values.
Canard taper and sweep are assumed.
The real Starship canard has variable sweep.
Cruise CL calculation assumes sea-level density and 90% MTOW.
This is not a validated aircraft performance prediction.
